# The context pack

What leaves this repository. Everything else here is for the people who maintain the
layer; a pack is for a consumer with none of the context, no access to the git history,
and a decision to make.

- `content.nt` - one observation as sorted N-Triples: the source, the reading, and the
  artifacts that reading saw. Canonical, so two producers of the same observation emit
  the same bytes.
- `manifest.json` - the graph, the vocabulary version to compile against, the source,
  the target, the observation, when it was made, when it stops being worth believing,
  why it should be believed, and what the content hashes to.

A pack carries no business fact. L1 has no observation behind it and no freshness to
state, and shipping the two together would stamp declared business truth with an expiry
date it has no way to honour.

Both files are generated by `just pack` and `just test` asserts they are exactly what
the accepted graph packs. Verify one with `semantic_layer.pack.verify`, against an
instant you supply rather than the clock; it refuses a pack that is stale, tampered
with, inconsistent with its own manifest, from another source, or about another target.
See [docs/l2-technical-layer.md](../../../docs/l2-technical-layer.md).
